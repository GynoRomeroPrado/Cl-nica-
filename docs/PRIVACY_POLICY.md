# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 2025**

## Introduction

Welcome to Health & Fitness ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

By using our application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information

We collect information that you provide directly to us:

- **Account Information**: Email address, name, date of birth
- **Profile Information**: Height, weight, gender, fitness goals, experience level
- **Health Data**: Workout history (exercises, sets, reps, weight lifted), medication schedules, supplement intake
- **Usage Data**: App interactions, features used, session duration

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use our app, we automatically collect:

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Log Data**: IP address, access times, app crashes, performance data
- **Location Data**: General location (city/country level) - NOT precise GPS location
- **Analytics Data**: Feature usage, user behavior patterns (anonymized)

### 1.3 Health Information

We collect sensitive health-related data:

- **Workout Data**: Exercise routines, sets, repetitions, weights, workout duration
- **Medication Data**: Medication names, dosages, schedules, intake logs (encrypted)
- **Physical Metrics**: Height, weight, body measurements
- **Progress Data**: Workout streaks, volume progression, personal records

**Important**: We DO NOT collect or store:
- Medical diagnoses or conditions
- Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA
- Prescription details beyond what you voluntarily enter
- Genetic or biometric data

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 Primary Uses

We use your information to:

- **Provide Services**: Enable workout tracking, medication reminders, health news curation
- **Personalization**: Recommend exercises, adjust workout difficulty, predict injury risk
- **Notifications**: Send medication reminders, workout alerts, achievement notifications
- **Analytics**: Improve app performance, understand feature usage, fix bugs
- **Machine Learning**: Generate personalized workout recommendations, progression predictions

### 2.2 Communication

We may use your email to send:

- Account notifications (security alerts, password resets)
- Service updates (new features, maintenance)
- Marketing communications (you can opt-out anytime)

### 2.3 Aggregated Data

We use anonymized, aggregated data for:

- Research on fitness trends
- Improving ML algorithms
- Industry benchmarking
- Public health insights

**Aggregated data cannot identify you personally.**

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 3.1 We DO NOT Sell Your Data

We will NEVER sell your personal information to third parties.

### 3.2 Service Providers

We share data with trusted service providers who help us operate:

- **Cloud Hosting**: Supabase (database), Railway/AWS (backend hosting)
- **Analytics**: Mixpanel (user behavior - anonymized)
- **Error Tracking**: Sentry (crash reports)
- **Content Delivery**: CloudFlare (CDN for media files)

All service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data.

### 3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law:

- Court orders or legal processes
- Protecting our rights or property
- Preventing fraud or security threats
- Complying with GDPR/CCPA requests

### 3.4 Business Transfers

If we undergo a merger, acquisition, or sale, your data may be transferred. You will be notified of any such change.

## 4. Data Security

### 4.1 Encryption

- **At Rest**: All sensitive data (medications, personal info) encrypted with AES-256
- **In Transit**: All data transmitted over TLS 1.3
- **Passwords**: Never stored; hashed with bcrypt

### 4.2 Security Measures

- Regular security audits
- Access controls and authentication
- Monitoring for suspicious activity
- Secure backup procedures

### 4.3 No Absolute Security

While we implement industry-standard security, no system is 100% secure. Use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

### 5.1 Right to Access

You can request a copy of all data we hold about you. We will provide it in JSON format within 30 days.

### 5.2 Right to Deletion

You can delete your account at any time:

- Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account
- All personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
- Anonymized workout data may be retained for research

### 5.3 Right to Rectification

You can update or correct your information anytime in the app settings.

### 5.4 Right to Portability

You can export your data in machine-readable format (JSON).

### 5.5 Right to Object

You can opt-out of:

- Marketing emails (unsubscribe link)
- Analytics tracking (Settings > Privacy)
- Push notifications (device settings)

### 5.6 Exercising Your Rights

Contact us at: **privacy@healthapp.com**

We will respond within 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA).

## 6. Data Retention

- **Active Accounts**: Data retained as long as your account is active
- **Deleted Accounts**: Personal data deleted within 30 days
- **Anonymized Data**: Workout statistics (anonymized) retained indefinitely for research
- **Backups**: Deleted data may persist in backups for up to 90 days

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our app is NOT intended for users under 13 years old.

- We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13
- If we discover such data, we will delete it immediately
- For users 13-16 (EU): Parental consent required per GDPR

If you believe we have collected data from a child, contact us immediately.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Our servers are located in the United States and European Union.

- **EU Users**: Data may be transferred to the US under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
- **Privacy Shield**: We comply with GDPR requirements for international transfers

## 9. Third-Party Links

Our app may link to external websites (health articles, research papers). We are NOT responsible for their privacy practices. Review their policies separately.

## 10. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal tracking technologies:

- **Essential Cookies**: Session management, authentication (cannot be disabled)
- **Analytics Cookies**: Mixpanel for usage analytics (can be opted out)
- **NO Advertising Cookies**: We do not track you for ads

To exercise these rights, email **privacy@healthapp.com**

## 12. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

- Right to lodge a complaint with your Data Protection Authority
